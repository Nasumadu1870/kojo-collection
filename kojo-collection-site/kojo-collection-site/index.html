A museum for one photographer.

Nhyiraba Asumadu Addo, known as Kojo, photographs the people around him at Stevens and at home: runways and dance floors, graduates in kente, quiet streets in Egypt and Hoboken. He shoots on his own Nikon D5300 with a Godox flash from his favourite camera shop in Tahrir Square.

Every label on this site gives the date, the camera and an ID, like f_skate24-06, so any picture can be found again.

How this museum started

In July 2023, Kojo opened a Photoshop file and named it “like my own art gallery, failed.” He was arranging photos of himself and his friends at the Metropolitan Museum of Art as if they already hung on its walls. The idea came from Virgil Abloh: once something is in a museum, the art world treats it as art.

This site is that file, finished.
